At its core, agentic commerce refers to autonomous, intelligent software agents that can perform tasks, make decisions, and interact on behalf of businesses or individuals. These aren't just advanced chatbots; they are sophisticated systems capable of complex reasoning, learning, and executing multi-step processes across various business functions. Think of them as highly specialized, always-on digital employees, equipped to handle everything from sales outreach to supply chain optimization.
