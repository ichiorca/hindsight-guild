# The State of Agentic Commerce: A Founder's Briefing on Recent Advancements

> From open protocols to new payment systems, the landscape for autonomous AI shoppers is solidifying. Here's what founders need to know about the multi-trillion dollar shift.


Agentic commerce is the next evolution of how we buy and sell online. It’s a shift from customers manually searching for products to autonomous AI agents that anticipate needs, evaluate options, and execute purchases on a user's behalf. This isn't a distant future—the foundational layers are being built right now by major players, and the market is projected to be massive. Deloitte estimates agentic commerce could drive up to $17.5 trillion in commerce by 2030. More specifically, the AI Agents in eCommerce market is forecast to grow from $3.6 billion in 2024 to over $282 billion by 2034, demonstrating the immense urgency and scale of this transition. This isn't just about chatbots; it's a fundamental rewiring of the digital economy.

For founders, this represents a fundamental change in the digital shelf. The last two years have seen a rapid acceleration in the development of the protocols, platforms, and players that will define this new era.

### The Compelling Economics: Why Agentic Commerce Matters Now

Beyond the futuristic vision, the immediate business case for integrating agentic capabilities is becoming undeniable. Early data reveals significant impacts on both revenue and operational efficiency. For instance, shoppers complete purchases 47% faster when assisted by AI, and importantly, returning customers spend 25% more. These are not marginal gains; they represent a step-change in conversion and loyalty.

On the cost side, the impact is just as profound. AI agents can resolve up to 93% of customer questions without human help. This extends to complex issues, with chatbots already resolving 17% of billing disputes and 58% of returns autonomously. For any founder focused on scaling efficiently, these metrics signal a clear imperative to invest in agent-ready infrastructure.

### A Timeline of Recent Leaps

The pace of innovation has been staggering. Here are some of the key milestones from the last 18 months that have built the foundation for this new market:

*   **February 2024:** Amazon launches **Rufus**, an AI-powered shopping assistant. This move signals a major retailer's entry into the space, validating the potential of conversational, agent-led discovery.
*   **September 2024:** Salesforce launches **Agentforce**, demonstrating the push from major CRM platforms to enhance customer experiences with integrated AI chatbots and tools.
*   **Late 2024:** Perplexity introduces instant purchasing with its **Perplexity Buy with Pro** feature, showing how discovery-focused AI models can directly evolve into transactional agents.
*   **November 2024:** Anthropic releases the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**, an open standard for connecting AI agents to external data. This is a critical foundational layer for allowing agents to access real-time, accurate information.
*   **January 2025:** OpenAI launches **Operator**, an agent integrated into ChatGPT to automate tasks like booking travel, showcasing the model's shift from answering questions to performing actions.
*   **April 2025:** Google introduces **Agent2Agent (A2A)**, an interoperability protocol for AI agents to communicate with each other. This points to a future where specialized agents collaborate to fulfill complex user requests.
*   **September 2025:** Stripe and OpenAI release the **Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP)** as an open standard (Apache 2.0) for enabling purchases within chat interfaces. In the same month, Google launches its **Agent Payments Protocol (AP2)** with early participants like Wayfair, Chewy, and Etsy, solidifying the push for standardized, secure transactional frameworks.
*   **January 2026:** An IBM study finds that 45% of consumers are already using AI in their buying journey, showing rapid consumer adoption of adjacent technologies and a growing comfort with AI-assisted commerce.

Looking ahead, key trends for 2025 and 2026 include the proliferation of specialized AI Shopping Agents, the deepening of Personalized Commerce Journeys, and crucial advancements in agent-managed Returns & Reverse Logistics and overall Supply Chain Autonomy.

### Beyond Protocols: Key Players Shaping the Agentic Landscape

While open protocols create the rules of the road, a diverse set of major players is building the highways. The ecosystem extends far beyond the AI labs. **Shopify** is actively developing agentic shopping infrastructure, positioning itself to allow agents to tap into its massive catalog and build carts across its millions of merchants. This is a crucial move to empower independent retailers in the new landscape.

Retail giants are also moving quickly. **Walmart**, for example, is piloting a conversational grocery assistant that uses natural language processing to help shoppers with specific brand preferences, indicating a focus on vertical-specific agent applications. Meanwhile, **Microsoft** is providing the picks and shovels for this gold rush. Retailers are using tools like Microsoft's Copilot Studio and Azure AI Agent Service to build, deploy, and scale their own high-quality autonomous agents, aiming to increase revenue and reduce operational costs.

### Key Strategic Takeaways for Founders

This isn't just a trend to watch; it's a structural shift to prepare for. Here’s what it means for your business:

1.  **Your Data Must Be Machine-Readable:** The single most critical preparation is to ensure your product data is impeccably structured and optimized for machines. AI agents won't browse your beautiful website; they will query your data directly. This means going beyond basic product feeds. Impeccably structured data includes real-time inventory levels accessible via API, standardized product attributes (e.g., size, color, material, compatibility), clear pricing and shipping information, and rich metadata that an agent can use to make informed comparisons. Think in terms of schemas, not just spreadsheets.

2.  **Open Protocols Are Defining the Playing Field:** The development of open standards like ACP and MCP is crucial. It suggests the industry is moving towards interoperability rather than closed ecosystems where a single large player dominates. For founders, this is an opportunity. By aligning with these emerging standards, you ensure your products can be discovered and purchased by any agent, on any platform, preventing vendor lock-in and maximizing your reach.

3.  **The Merchant of Record Remains Key:** While agents will orchestrate transactions, early indications from protocol development show that merchants will likely remain the merchant of record. This is a critical point. Maintaining this status means you continue to own the customer relationship, control your branding and pricing, and retain access to vital post-sale data for analytics and retargeting. The goal is to allow agents to access your store as a new sales channel, not to become an invisible backend for a single, massive AI platform.

4.  **Balance Innovation with Trust:** Widespread adoption of fully autonomous purchasing will take time. Building consumer and merchant trust is essential. This can be done by implementing 'human-in-the-loop' systems where agents suggest purchases for user approval, rather than executing them unilaterally. Be transparent about why an agent recommends a particular product and provide clear explanations of how user data is being used and protected. Reliability and security, especially around payment information, are non-negotiable.

The groundwork for agentic commerce is being laid now. For founders, the imperative is clear: structure your data for AI, watch the development of open protocols, and build a strategy to thrive in a world where your next customer might be a machine.
